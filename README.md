### Definition of Prompting:

Prompting is the process of giving instructions, questions, or input text (called a prompt) to an AI system so that it generates the desired output. The quality of the prompt directly affects the quality, accuracy, and relevance of the AI's response.

In Prompt Engineering, different prompting techniques are used to guide AI models effectively for various tasks such as answering questions, generating content, summarizing information, and solving problems.

---
### 1. Straightforward Prompting:
Straightforward Prompting is the simplest form of prompting where the user directly asks the AI to perform a task without providing additional context, examples, or formatting instructions.

## Example:
### Prompt 1:
Explain the importance of cybersecurity in two sentences.

### Output:
Cybersecurity protects computer systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access and attacks. It helps ensure privacy, security, and business continuity.

### Prompt 2:
Cybersecurity protects computer systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access and attacks. It helps ensure privacy, security, and business continuity.

### Output:
1. Cost savings
2. Scalability
3. Remote accessibility
4. Data backup and recovery

### Prompt 3:
Define photosynthesis in one sentence.

### Output:
Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to produce food and oxygen.

### Prompt 4:
List three advantages of electric vehicles.

### Output:
1. Reduced air pollution.
2. Lower fuel and maintenance costs.
3. Improved energy efficiency.

### Evaluation:

### Strengths:

Easy to create and understand.
Requires minimal effort.
Suitable for simple tasks.

### Weaknesses:

Output may be too general.
Lack of structure.
Results may vary depending on interpretation.

### Advantages:

Quick response generation.
User-friendly for beginners.
Effective for simple information requests.

### Limitations:

Less control over output format.
May miss important details.
Inconsistent responses for complex tasks.

### Conclusion:

Straightforward prompting is useful for obtaining quick answers but provides limited control over response quality and structure.

Tabular Format Prompting instructs the AI to present information in a table with rows and columns for easy comparison and analysis.

| Performance       | Moderate | High      |
| Power Consumption | Low      | Higher    |
| Upgrade Options   | Limited  | Extensive |

### Prompt 2:
Provide a table of five database management systems and their applications.

### Output:
| DBMS       | Type       | Application           |
| ---------- | ---------- | --------------------- |
| MySQL      | Relational | Web Applications      |
| Oracle     | Relational | Enterprise Systems    |
| MongoDB    | NoSQL      | Big Data Applications |
| PostgreSQL | Relational | Data Analytics        |
| SQLite     | Embedded   | Mobile Applications   |

Compare and contrast AC and DC current in a table.

| Feature           | AC (Alternating Current)       | DC (Direct Current)    |

### Evaluation:

### Strengths:

Information is well organized.

Better visualization of data.

Limited descriptive detail.

Tabular prompting is effective for comparisons and structured information presentation.

### 3. Missing Word Prompting:
Missing Word Prompting provides incomplete information with a blank or missing word. The AI is asked to predict or complete the missing information based on context.

## Example:
### Prompt 1:
Complete the sentence: The largest planet in the solar system is ______.

### Output:
Jupiter

### Prompt 2:
Complete the sentence: A computer's brain is called the ______.

### Output:
CPU (Central Processing Unit)

### Prompt 3:
The capital of France is ____.

### Output:
Paris

### Prompt 4:
In photosynthesis, plants absorb sunlight to produce ____.

### Output:
food (glucose)

### Evaluation:

### Strengths:

Tests contextual understanding.
Useful for learning and assessments.
Helps in language training.

### Weaknesses:

Limited to short responses.
Not suitable for detailed explanations.

### Advantages:

Interactive learning.
Quick evaluation of knowledge.
Easy to create.

### Limitations:

Less effective for complex tasks.
Depends on the clarity of the context.

### Conclusion:

Missing Word Prompting is valuable for educational activities, quizzes, and knowledge assessment.

---
### 4. Preceding Question Prompting:
Preceding Question Prompting asks the AI to consider an initial question or context before answering the main question. This helps establish the situation and can make the final response more relevant.

## Example:
### Prompt 1:
Why is artificial intelligence important in healthcare? Explain its major applications.

### Output:
Artificial intelligence helps improve diagnosis, treatment planning, and patient monitoring. Applications include medical imaging, disease prediction, virtual assistants, and drug discovery.

### Prompt 2:
Why is cybersecurity important? What are the common methods used to protect computer systems?

### Output:
Cybersecurity is important because it protects data, networks, and systems from cyber threats and unauthorized access. Common protection methods include firewalls, antivirus software, encryption, strong passwords, and multi-factor authentication.

### Prompt 3:
Why is climate change a global concern? Explain how greenhouse gases contribute to global warming.

### Output:
Climate change is a global concern because it affects weather patterns, ecosystems, agriculture, and human health worldwide. Greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide and methane trap heat in the Earth's atmosphere, causing global temperatures to rise and leading to global warming.

### Prompt 4:
How do vaccines work? Describe the process of immunization in simple terms.

### Output:
Vaccines work by training the immune system to recognize and fight harmful germs. During immunization, a weakened or inactive form of a pathogen is introduced into the body, allowing the immune system to create antibodies and memory cells that provide protection against future infections.

### Evaluation:

### Strengths:

Produces more focused responses.
Encourages logical reasoning.
Provides better context.

### Weaknesses:

Requires multiple questions.
Slightly increases prompt length.

### Advantages:

Better accuracy.
Improved coherence.
Suitable for analytical tasks.

### Limitations:

More time needed to design prompts.
Can become complex if too many questions are included.

### Conclusion:

Preceding Question Prompting enhances AI understanding by building context before requesting the final answer.
